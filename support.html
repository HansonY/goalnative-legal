常见问题

  • 怎么开始? 用 Apple 登录 → 在「创作」用一句话造一门课,或在「学习」上传 PDF 教材;然后到「背诵」逐句开口背,≥80 分过关。
  • 背诵怎么算过关? 逐句开口背,系统按语音识别打分,全课平均 ≥80 即过关、计入你的累计分钟。
  • 订阅(Goal Native Pro)怎么管理或取消? iPhone「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」里可随时管理或取消;App 内付费墙也有「恢复购买」。
  • 录音会上传吗? 不会。背诵录音只保存在你的设备本地。详见隐私政策。
  • 怎么删除账号? 「我的 → 删除账号」可随时删除账号及服务器数据。

FAQ

  • How do I start? Sign in with Apple → create a course from one sentence in "Create", or upload a PDF textbook in "Learning"; then go to "Recite" and say it aloud sentence by sentence (pass at ≥80).
  • How does recitation pass? Recite each sentence aloud; the app scores you by speech recognition. A course average of ≥80 passes and counts toward your recited minutes.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription (Goal Native Pro)? On iPhone: Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. The paywall also has "Restore Purchases".
  • Are my recordings uploaded? No. Recitation recordings stay on your device only. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete Account removes your account and server data anytime.
