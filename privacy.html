Goal Native 隐私政策

最后更新:2026-07-24

Goal Native(以下称"本应用")是一款英语背诵学习应用。我们重视你的隐私,本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、如何使用,以及你的选择。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 账号信息:你通过"使用 Apple 登录"注册。我们仅接收 Apple 提供的用户标识,以及你选择分享的姓名/邮箱(可为 Apple 隐私中继邮箱)。我们不收集密码。
  • 学习数据:你创建/加入的课程、学习进度、背诵成绩与累计时长、复习计划等,用于提供学习功能并在你的账号下同步。
  • 你上传的教材:PDF 教材在你的设备本地进行文字识别(OCR),识别出的文本用于生成课程;原始 PDF 不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 麦克风与语音:背诵/跟读时使用麦克风,语音识别在设备本机进行(Apple 语音框架)用于评分。录音文件只保存在你的设备本地,不会上传。

2. 我们如何使用信息

  • 提供并同步学习功能(课程、进度、背诵记录)。
  • 用 AI 生成课程内容与语音(文字转语音)。
  • 管理订阅状态与配额。
  • 保障安全、排查故障、改进产品。

我们不将你的个人信息出售给第三方,不用于广告追踪。

3. 第三方服务

  • Apple:登录(Sign in with Apple)、订阅与支付(App 内购买)。支付信息由 Apple 处理,我们不接触你的支付卡信息。
  • Supabase:提供账号鉴权、数据库与存储(托管你的学习数据)。
  • AI 服务提供商:为生成课程文本/语音,会把你的造课主题或教材文本发送给第三方 AI 接口处理。

4. 数据存储与保留

学习数据保存在你的账号下,直到你删除。录音等敏感文件仅存于设备本地。

5. 你的权利

  • 删除账号:在"我的 → 删除账号"可随时删除账号及关联的服务器数据。
  • 退出登录:随时退出。
  • 撤回权限:可在系统"设置"中关闭麦克风/语音识别权限。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向收集个人信息。

7. 政策变更

如有重大变更,我们会更新本页并修改"最后更新"日期。

8. 联系我们

有任何隐私问题,请联系:handsomeyang@qq.com

Goal Native Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-24

Goal Native ("the App") is an English recitation-learning app. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account: You sign up via Sign in with Apple. We receive your Apple user identifier and any name/email you choose to share (which may be an Apple private-relay email). We do not collect passwords.
  • Learning data: Courses you create/join, progress, recitation scores and accumulated time, and review schedules — used to provide learning features and sync under your account.
  • Materials you upload: PDF text recognition (OCR) runs on your device; the recognized text is used to generate courses. The original PDF is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Microphone & speech: Used during recitation/shadowing. Speech recognition runs on-device (Apple Speech framework) for scoring. Audio recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded.

2. How We Use It

  • Provide and sync learning features.
  • Generate course content and text-to-speech via AI.
  • Manage subscription status and quotas.
  • Security, troubleshooting, and product improvement.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, subscriptions and payments (In-App Purchase). Payment details are handled by Apple; we never access your card information.
  • Supabase: Authentication, database and storage for your learning data.
  • AI providers: To generate course text/audio, your course topic or material text is sent to third-party AI APIs for processing.

4. Storage & Retention

Learning data is retained under your account until you delete it. Sensitive files such as recordings remain on your device only.

5. Your Rights

  • Delete account: Profile → Delete Account removes your account and associated server data at any time.
  • Sign out anytime.
  • Revoke permissions: Disable microphone/speech recognition in system Settings.

6. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date for material changes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact handsomeyang@qq.com.
